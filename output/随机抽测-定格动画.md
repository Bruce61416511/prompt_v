Style: STOP-MOTION ANIMATION — stepped, frame-by-frame motion. True 12fps, ANIMATED ON TWOS: 12 distinct poses per second, each pose held two frames then snapping to the next, never gliding. A handmade CLAYMATION look — soft plasticine with visible fingerprint smudges, tool marks and slightly uneven hand-rolled edges, NOT paper, NOT felt, NOT felted wool, NOT 3D CG, NOT smooth digital render. Constant tactile BOIL — the clay surface and outlines subtly alive frame to frame. NO smooth interpolation, NO motion blur, NO morphing. Real frame-by-frame animation, not AI slop.

SUBJECT: a small clay fox, roughly palm-sized, built from warm orange plasticine with a cream-white chest and tail tip, tiny black bead eyes, a stubby hand-pinched snout, slightly crooked ears that wobble when it moves. Its four legs are separate rolled clay pieces with visible seams at the joints. It carries a tiny woven clay basket on its back.

SETTING: a miniature autumn forest floor diorama on a tabletop — a floor of real dried oak and maple leaves, curled and brittle, with acorns, twigs, moss patches and small clay mushrooms scattered around. Warm amber, russet and ochre palette, soft-box studio lighting from above, tilt-shift shallow depth of field, miniature diorama scale.

CAMERA: perfectly locked low tabletop stage, slightly above eye level of the fox, no camera movement, no zoom, no cuts, no human hands, no surrounding objects outside the diorama. Only the fox and the leaves it touches change. The camera holds one continuous static frame.

SEGMENT-BY-SEGMENT ACTION:
1. The clay fox steps forward on twos, one stiff leg at a time, body bobbing with each held pose, exaggerated pose amplitude so the stepped cadence reads clearly on every step.
2. It stops, tilts its head sharply to one side, sniffs — the snout dips twice in two distinct held poses.
3. It spots an acorn under a curled leaf, pounces: a big exaggerated leap, all four legs splayed, held mid-air for two frames as one frozen pose, then snapping down.
4. It lands, nose-first, and the leaf flips over with a stiff two-frame rotation.
5. It picks up the acorn, tucks it into the basket, and trots off frame with the same stepped bob.

ATMOSPHERIC SEPARATION: one or two loose leaves and a thin drift of dust motes may fall SMOOTHLY and continuously in the background — everything else, the fox, the acorns, the leaves it touches, steps on twos. Do not let the background motion smooth out the fox.

CRAFT IMPERFECTIONS (required): tiny positional jitter between poses, an occasional one-frame motion smear on the fastest pounce, slightly uneven hand-shaped clay edges, a faint fingerprint ridge catching the light on the fox's flank.

LIGHT: warm soft-box key from above, gentle falloff, soft contact shadows under the fox and the acorns, no god rays, no dramatic beams.

NEGATIVE: smooth interpolation, motion blur, morphing, CGI plastic surfaces, paper or felt texture, moving camera, zoom, cuts, human hands, cartoon facial expressions, extra limbs, floating objects, text, watermark, oversaturated colors.

## 生成后请检查这三条

1. **顿挫感是否真的在**——暂停任意两帧，狐狸的姿势应该是"跳"过去的，不是"滑"过去的；如果动作连贯顺滑，说明步进被抹平了。
2. **材质是否被混掉**——狐狸表面应能看到指纹压痕和手工不齐的边缘；如果变成光滑塑料或毛毡质感，重跑一次并在开头加重 `NOT paper, NOT felt, NOT 3D CG`。
3. **机位是否锁死**——全程应无推拉摇移、无镜头切换、无人类的手入画；一旦出现运镜，说明定格与运镜的互斥需求里运镜赢了，需要重写。
